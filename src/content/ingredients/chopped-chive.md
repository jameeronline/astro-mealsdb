Chopped chive is the finely cut, green, hollow leaves of the chive plant, a member of the allium family that also includes garlic, onions, and leeks. It has a mild, onion-like flavor and is used as a garnish or ingredient to add fresh taste and color to many dishes, from eggs and potatoes to creamy sauces and soups.
