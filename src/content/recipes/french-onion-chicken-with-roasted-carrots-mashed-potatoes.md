1. 1
Preheat oven to 425 degrees.
2. Wash and dry all produce.
3. Trim, peel, and cut carrots on a diagonal into ¼-inch-thick pieces.
4. Dice potatoes into ½-inch pieces.
5. Halve, peel, and thinly slice onion.
2
Toss carrots on a baking sheet with a drizzle of oil, salt, and pepper.
6. Roast until browned and tender, 15-20 minutes.
3
Meanwhile, place potatoes in a medium pot with enough salted water to cover by 2 inches.
7. Bring to a boil and cook until tender, 12-15 minutes.
8. Drain and return potatoes to pot; cover to keep warm.
4
While potatoes cook, heat a drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
9. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and softened, 8-10 minutes.
10. Sprinkle with 1 tsp sugar (2 tsp for 4 servings).
11. Stir in stock concentrate and 2 TBSP water (¼ cup for 4); season with salt and pepper.
12. Cook until jammy, 2-3 minutes more.
13. Turn off heat; transfer to a small bowl.
14. Wash out pan.
5
Pat chicken dry with paper towels; season all over with salt and pepper.
15. Heat a drizzle of oil in pan used for onion over medium-high heat.
16. Add chicken and cook until browned and cooked through, 5-6 minutes per side.
17. In the last 1-2 minutes of cooking, top with caramelized onion and cheese.
18. Cover pan until cheese melts. (If your pan doesn’t have a lid, cover with a baking sheet!)
6
Heat pot with drained potatoes over low heat; mash with sour cream, 2 TBSP butter (4 TBSP for 4 servings), salt, pepper, and a splash of water (or milk, for extra richness) until smooth.
19. Divide chicken, roasted carrots, and mashed potatoes between plates.
