1. Serves 2
Adjust racks to top and middle positions and preheat oven to 450 degrees.
2. Wash and dry all produce.
3. Dice sweet potatoes into 1/2-inch pieces.
4. Toss on a baking sheet with a drizzle of oil, salt, and pepper.
5. Roast on top rack for 12 minutes (we'll roast the zucchini then).
6. Meanwhile, halve and core apple; thinly slice into half-moons.
7. Peel and finely chop garlic.
8. Quarter lemon.
9. Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; cut crosswise into 1/2-inch-thick half-moons.
10. Toss on a second baking sheet with a drizzle of oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.
11. Set aside.
12. Pat pork dry with paper towels and season all over with salt and pepper.
13. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
14. Add pork and cook until browned and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side.
15. Turn off heat; transfer to a plate.
16. Once sweet potatoes have roasted 12 minutes, transfer baking sheet with zucchini to middle rack and continue roasting until both veggies are browned and softened, 12-15 minutes more.
17. Meanwhile, melt 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings) in pan used for pork over medium-high heat.
18. Add apple and season with salt and pepper.
19. Cook, scraping up any browned bits from bottom of pan, until apple is slightly softened, 2-3 minutes.
20. Add garlic; cook until fragrant, 30 seconds.
21. Add 1/z cup water (3/4 cup for 4), stock concentrate, and 11/2 tsp sugar (3 tsp for 4).
22. Cook, stirring, until sauce has thickened and apple is very tender, 3-5 minutes.
23. Season with salt and pepper.
24. Remove pan with apple from heat; stir in 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings) and a squeeze of lemon juice.
25. Divide pork, zucchini, and sweet potatoes between plates.
26. Top pork with glazed apple sauce.
27. Top zucchini with a squeeze of lemon juice.
