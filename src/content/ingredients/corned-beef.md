Corned beef is salt-cured beef, traditionally a brisket, that has been preserved in a seasoned brine. The name "corned" comes from the large, "corn-sized" grains of salt historically used. It is now a popular dish often prepared by slow cooking or boiling, and its salty, savory flavor makes it a key ingredient in sandwiches and hashes.
