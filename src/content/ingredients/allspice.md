Allspice is a spice from the dried berry of the Pimenta dioica tree, known for its flavor that combines notes of cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, and black pepper. It is used in both sweet and savory dishes, such as jerk chicken, mulled drinks, gingerbread, and stews, and is also called pimento or Jamaican pepper. Allspice is available whole or ground, and whole berries should be removed before serving if used in soups or stews.
