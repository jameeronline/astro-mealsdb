1. ▢
Heat olive oil in a large pot.
2. Cut beef into 1 inch (2.5 cm) cubes and brown in oil.
▢
Dice the onion and add to browning beef.
3. Add the stock and bring to a boil, then lower the heat, cover, and let simmer for about 30 minutes.
▢
Peel and cut the rutabaga and celery root into 1 inch (2.5 cm) cubes.
4. Add to the pot, cover, and continue simmering for another 30 minutes.
▢
Peel and chop the rest of the vegetables into 1 inch (2.5 cm) cubes and add to the pot.
5. Slice the leek into rings.
6. Cover and continue simmering for about 20 minutes.
7. Stir as little as possible.
▢
Top with fresh parsley.
