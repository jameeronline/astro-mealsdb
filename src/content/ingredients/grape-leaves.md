Grape leaves are the young, edible leaves from a grapevine, widely used in Mediterranean, Balkan, Middle Eastern, and Caucasus cuisines for dishes like dolma (stuffed grape leaves). They are prepared fresh or preserved in jars and offer a unique, tangy, citrusy flavor to recipes with a tender texture when cooked.
