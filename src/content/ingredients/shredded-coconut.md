 Shredded coconut is the white flesh of a mature coconut, which has been finely grated into thin, flexible strips. It is a versatile ingredient used in both sweet and savory dishes for adding flavor and chewy texture. The product can be found in sweetened or unsweetened varieties, with the unsweetened version being suitable for savory applications and the sweetened one being best for desserts.
