Ancho chillies are poblano peppers that have been roasted and dried. They have a sweet and smoky flavour which adds richness to cooking. They have a mild heat and are reminiscent to raisins in flavour. These flakes are perfect for sprinkling over an array of dishes including salads, soups, tacos and much more.
