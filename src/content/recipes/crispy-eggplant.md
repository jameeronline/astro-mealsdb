1. After 30 minutes wash eggplant slices from salt and pat dry with a kitchen towel.
2. In a large bowl/plate place breadcrumbs and sesame seeds. In another bowl beat 2 eggs with pinch salt and pepper.
3. Heal oil in a large skillet over high heat.
4. Dip eggplant slices in egg, then in crumbs, and place in hot oil. Fry 2 to 3 minutes on each side, or until golden brown. Drain on a paper towel.
