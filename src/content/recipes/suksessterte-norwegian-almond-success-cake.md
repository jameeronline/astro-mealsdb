1. Almond base
▢
Preheat oven to 345°F (175°C) and grease cake form (I use a 27 cm spring form) – you can line the bottom with baking paper if you like.
▢
Grind almonds in an almond grinder or food processor. I like to keep them a bit coarse.
▢
Gently whisk the eggs and sugar together until combined (no longer).
2. Stir in the ground almonds, flour, and baking powder.
▢
Pour batter into cake form and bake for about 45 – 50 minutes, monitoring to make sure the top doesn't burn.
3. Yellow egg cream
▢
Add egg yolks, heavy cream, sugar, and vanilla to a saucepan.
4. Heat over low/medium heat while stirring constantly until the mixture thickens – about 15 minutes.
5. Turn up the heat if the mixture doesn't thicken, but be careful not to boil.
▢
Let the mixture cool to room temperature and then add the butter.
6. You can use an electric mixer for a fluffy egg cream.
7. Assembly
▢
Wait for the cake to cool completely before removing from form and frosting.
