1. In a medium bowl, combine hulled and quartered strawberries, 4 Tbsp sugar and 4 Tbsp liqueur, stir to combine then cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour and up to 2 hours, stirring once or twice.

Just before serving, in a large mixing bowl, combine 1 cup cold heavy cream and 1/4 cup powdered sugar, and beat with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.
3. Using a spatula, fold in 1/4 cup sour cream just until well blended.
4. To serve, stir strawberries then divide between 6 serving glasses or bowls.
5. You can spoon a little syrup over the berries if you like.
6. You can also use this syrup to soak a cake.
7. Spoon cream over strawberries, dividing evenly.
8. You can also use an ice cream scoop with trigger release for a nice rounded puff of cream.
9. Serve right away or chill and enjoy within 2 hours of assembly.
