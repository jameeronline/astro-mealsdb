1. ▢
Cook the sour cream in a covered saucepan on medium heat for about 5 minutes.
▢
Turn down the heat and add half of the flour and stir well with a whisk.
2. Once the flour is fully incorporated, let the mixture continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until fat starts to release.
3. Use a spoon to gather as much of the fat as you can in a small bowl, saving for later. (Don't worry if you can't get any fat – in that case you can add butter later.)
▢
Whisk in the rest of the flour and then slowly add the milk, whisking constantly to avoid lumps.
4. Let the porridge continue to cook on low heat for 5 minutes and then add salt.
▢
Serve with sugar, cinnamon, and the fat from the porridge.
5. If you're using lower fat sour cream you can top the rømmegrøt with some butter instead.
