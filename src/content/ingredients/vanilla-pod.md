A vanilla pod is the fruit of the vanilla orchid, a long, dark, and wrinkled bean that contains thousands of tiny seeds. It is a prized ingredient for its sweet, fragrant flavor, which is created through a labor-intensive, multi-step curing process that transforms the green, unscented pods into the aromatic spice used in baking and cooking.
